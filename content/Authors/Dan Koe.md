# Dan Koe

Dan Koe (@thedankoe) is a writer and content creator who advocates for essay writing as the antidote to the degradation of collective thinking. He frames content creation through the lens of civilizational impact and the [[The Meaning Economy|meaning economy]].

## Key Ideas

- The [[Epistemic Commons]] is being poisoned by engagement-optimized content
- [[Fast Content vs Slow Content]] as a framework for evaluating media
- [[Essays as Thinking Tools]] — essays as the last form AI cannot replicate
- **Value creators** — ordinary people who make sense of their own minds and document them in public, distinct from influencers or personal brands
- [[The Meaning Economy]] — meaning as the scarcest commodity, created through ordered consciousness
- [[Creativity as a State]] — creativity is not a talent but a natural state of consciousness buried by conditioning, productivity pressure, and infinite input
- [[Default Mode Network]] and [[Hedonic Adaptation]] — the neuroscience behind creative burnout and recovery
- [[Reticular Activating System]] — why a meaningful project is the essential frame for consistent creative output
- [[Techno-Economic Evolution]] — stages of societal development (premodern → metamodern) and the corresponding evolution of meaning-making
- [[The Sovereign Individual Triad]] — self-education, self-interest, self-sufficiency; the three conditions that produce a sovereign individual and naturally generate a generalist
- [[The Development-Based Creator Model]] — two paths (skill-based vs development-based); turn yourself into the customer avatar; brand as environment; systems as product
- [[Agency]] — the most important skill; three traits (iterate without permission, experiment, believe in the difficult); agency as art form; how to practice it
- [[Learned Helplessness]] — Seligman's dog experiment; why people don't attempt difficult goals even when escape is available
- [[Generalists vs Specialists]] — tools evolve, vision doesn't; the Prussian education model; Shakespeare as synthesizer
- [[The 5 Human Capabilities]] — computation, transformation, variation, selection, attention; why AGI doesn't make humans irrelevant
- [[Identity-First Change]] — all behavior is goal-oriented; real change requires changing identity, not discipline; the 8-step anatomy of identity
- [[Ego Development Stages]] — the full 9-stage map from Impulsive to Unitive; the life you want requires a specific level of mind
- [[Cybernetics]] — the art of getting what you want; intelligence as the ability to iterate and learn from mistakes
- [[The Video Game Framework]] — anti-vision, vision, 1-year goal, 1-month project, daily levers, constraints
- [[The Swap Test]] — if the creator could be swapped out and the creation would be equally valuable, AI can replace it
- [[The Post-Labor Skill Stack]] — the five-level hierarchy: agency, taste, perspective, persuasion, technical know-how

## Philosophy

Koe occupies an interesting position: pro-AI but deeply concerned about what it does to creativity and genuine thinking. He sees the future of content belonging to "slow content" — essays, long-form thinking — precisely because AI makes everything else trivially easy to produce.

His concept of the "value creator" reframes content creation as civilizational responsibility: information influences identity, identity influences behavior, behavior influences flourishing or destruction.
