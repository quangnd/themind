# 7 Ideas to Launch Into a New Life in 2026

**Author:** Dan Koe (@thedankoe)
**Source:** The Dan Koe Letter (newsletter)

## Summary

A comprehensive guide to identity-level behavior change — arguing that most attempts to change fail because they target actions (second-order) instead of identity (first-order). Koe synthesizes developmental psychology, cybernetics, and teleology into a one-day protocol for psychological excavation and life reorientation.

Structured in seven sections:
1. **Identity-first change** — you must become the person before you achieve the outcome
2. **All behavior is goal-oriented** — even procrastination and self-sabotage are purposeful; real change requires changing your goals, not your discipline
3. **The anatomy of identity** — conditioning, fear, and the 8-step identity cycle
4. **Ego development stages** — 9 stages from Impulsive to Unitive; the life you want requires a specific level of mind
5. **Cybernetics and intelligence** — Naval's definition; the goal-act-sense-compare loop; high intelligence = ability to iterate and learn from mistakes
6. **The one-day Life Launch Protocol** — psychological excavation (anti-vision + vision), autopilot interrupts, synthesis
7. **The Video Game Framework** — anti-vision, vision, 1-year goal, 1-month project, daily levers, constraints

## Key Claims

- If you want a specific outcome, you must have the lifestyle that creates that outcome long before you reach it
- All behavior is teleological: even destructive behavior is achieving an unconscious goal
- The anatomy of identity is an 8-step cycle: goal → perception → learning → action → conditioning → identity → defense → new goals
- Moving through ego development stages requires allowing disorientation rather than resolving it quickly
- High intelligence = iterating and learning from mistakes; low intelligence = getting stuck and quitting
- Goals are not targets but lenses — they determine what you notice and how you interpret reality
- The Video Game Framework turns life into an obsession engine by providing: win condition (vision), stakes (anti-vision), mission (1-year), boss fight (1-month), quests (daily levers), rules (constraints)
