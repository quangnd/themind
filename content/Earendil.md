# Earendil

A Vienna-based AI company building agent-powered consumer software. Named after Tolkien's star-sailor. Home of [[Pi (Coding Agent)]] and its products. Founded by [[Armin Ronacher]] and Colin (last name not disclosed), with [[Mario Zechner]] joining in April 2026 as a shareholder and lead of pi.

Website: [earendil.com](https://earendil.com)
## Products

- **[[Pi (Coding Agent)]]** — the minimal coding agent at the technical core of everything Earendil builds
- **Elwing** — an email agent; the first consumer-facing product; each team member built their own version ("demented elf" was Colin's, now retired)
- **[[OpenClaw]]** — built by Peter Steinberger on top of pi; went viral; what catalyzed Mario joining

## Philosophy

Earendil has a [published charter of values](https://earendil.com/values/). In Mario's words: "well-meaning hippies who think software, and specifically AI, should serve humans, not the other way around." Key orientation:
- Software should enable humans, not replace them
- OSS and open protocols as necessity, not just optics
- Family-conscious work culture (multiple team members have kids; explicitly factored into how the company operates)

Mario's caveat: charters are only as good as the people behind them. He judges by track record and shared history, not the document.

## Team (as of April 2026)

- **Colin** — co-founder; ex-finance, product sense, handles the parts Mario doesn't want
- **[[Mario Zechner]]** — shareholder and pi lead; joined April 2026
- **Cristina** — engineer; first early hire; mentored by Peter Steinberger
- Jakob, Ramiz, Vegard — engineers; all generalists

## Pi Governance at Earendil

Pi is owned by Earendil. Mario holds full technical direction over pi alongside Armin and Colin. Governance model:
- External contributions: no CLA, no DCO — PRs reviewed and merged as before
- Trademark: Pi name and logo trademarked by Earendil (Mozilla/Linux model) — if you see pi, it's an Earendil product
- Licensing: three-tier model (MIT core / Fair Source value-add / Proprietary enterprise). See [[Pi (Coding Agent)]] for details.
- Fork protection: "if you ever feel like we've lost the plot, the fork button on GitHub still works"

## Funding

Early-stage investors with dev-tools experience. Mario became a small investor prior to joining. No VC names disclosed publicly.

## Context

The path to Earendil ran through the [[Vienna School of Agentic Coding]] — the loose technical friendship of Mario, Armin, and Peter Steinberger. OpenClaw's viral growth brought inbound interest that forced the question of what to do with pi, and Earendil's offer gave Mario the answer that fit his constraints.
