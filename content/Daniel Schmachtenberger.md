# Daniel Schmachtenberger

Daniel Schmachtenberger is a systems thinker who has dedicated his life to studying the [[Metacrisis]] — the convergence of interconnected civilizational threats. [[Dan Koe]] describes him as "one of the most important thinkers of our time."

## Profile

Schmachtenberger is known for being calm, nuanced, and non-polarizing. He doesn't post much publicly but makes occasional appearances on podcasts where his depth of thinking becomes immediately apparent.

## Key Ideas

- **The [[Metacrisis]]** — the underlying pattern that generates all civilizational crises
- **Generator functions** — three forces (rivalrous dynamics, substrate consumption, exponential technology) that drive societies toward catastrophe
- **Three attractors** — the basins complex societies tend toward: collapse, dystopian control, or aligned civilization
- **Sense-making** — the capacity for shared understanding as prerequisite for solving civilizational problems

## Notable Quotes (via Koe)

> The written word as the primary type of media was probably required for democracy to work, because it required the capacity to pay attention to an idea for long enough to understand it.

> As technology is empowering our choices and we are getting something like the power of gods, you have to have something like the love and the wisdom of gods to wield that or you self-destruct.

> Wisdom is not algorithmic and cannot be made algorithmic.
