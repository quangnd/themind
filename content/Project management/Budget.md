Budget is a plan for how to spend money.

It tells you:

- How much money you have (for project).
- What's you'll spend it on (wood, nails, snacks).

![[Attachments/Budget_of_project.png]]

Having a budget plan helps you avoid running of money before the treehouse is done.

When budget and [[Schedule]] are fixed, unexpected change can be managed by varying [[Scope of the project]]
