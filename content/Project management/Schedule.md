Schedule is a time map. It tells you **what to do first**, next, last and **when to do it**.

![[Attachments/Schedule_of_project.png]]

> Schedule = a roadmap for timing tasks

A treehouse build schedule:

- First: build a ladder at 9:00 AM
- Next: attach roof at at 11:00 AM
- Last: paint it at 12:10 AM

Without a schedule, everyone might start painting treehouse before the ladder is ready! The schedule keep things organized so the treehouse gets **finished on time**.
